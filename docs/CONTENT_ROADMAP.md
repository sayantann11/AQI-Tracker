# AirScope India — Content Strategy & Expansion Roadmap

This content calendar and expansion roadmap outlines upcoming educational, seasonal, and regional content assets to sustain domain authority and organic traffic growth throughout the year.

---

## 1. Content Architecture & Quality Principles

All AirScope content adheres to Google’s E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness) standards:
- **Primary Sources First:** Cite official CPCB documents, MoEFCC gazettes, WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines 2021, and peer-reviewed scientific studies (IIT Kanpur, IIT Delhi, SAFAR).
- **No Machine Stacking:** Every article provides contextual explanation, diagrams, and actionable protection advice rather than generic AI summaries.
- **Strict Data Integrity:** Real measurements, actual physical units (&micro;g/m&sup3;, mg/m&sup3;), verified station coordinates, and genuine public health protocols.

## 2. Planned Editorial Pipeline (Months 1–6)

### Phase 1: High Smog Season (November – January)
*Theme: Immediate Health Protection & Emergency Protocols*
1. **The GRAP (Graded Response Action Plan) Complete Guide:**
   - Detailed breakdown of GRAP Stages I, II, III, and IV.
   - When do schools close, when do construction bans take effect, and what vehicle curbs apply?
2. **N95, N99 vs. Cloth & Surgical Masks: The Independent Test Guide:**
   - Why exhalation valves are banned, how electrostatic melt-blown filtration works, and proper seal checks.
3. **Indoor Air Purifier Buying Guide for Delhi NCR Homes:**
   - Calculating CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate) for room square footage. True HEPA (H13) vs Ionic generators (why ozone emission must be avoided).

### Phase 2: Post-Winter Transition (February – April)
*Theme: Dust Storms, Ozone Surges & Summer Air Dynamics*
4. **Ground-Level Ozone (O3) in Delhi Summer:**
   - Why ground-level ozone replaces PM2.5 as the primary urban respiratory hazard during hot sunny months.
5. **Western Dust Incursions & Aravalli Deforestation:**
   - How Thar desert dust storms elevate PM10 during May and June across Gurugram, Delhi, and Noida.

### Phase 3: Regional Expansion (Months 4–6)
*Theme: Scaling Beyond Delhi NCR across India*
6. **Mumbai vs. Delhi AQI Dynamics:**
   - Why sea breezes offer temporary relief to coastal cities, yet winter inversions still create severe local hotspots.
7. **Kolkata & Indo-Gangetic Basin Air Quality:**
   - Industrial and biomass emission profiles across West Bengal and Bihar.

## 3. Evergreen Interactive Content Assets

1. **AQI Health Risk Calculator:**
   - Interactive widget allowing users to enter age, pre-existing conditions (asthma, COPD, cardiovascular), and outdoor activity type to receive a personalized safety score.
2. **Hourly Pollution Curve Visualizer:**
   - Dynamic chart showing the standard 24-hour diurnal inversion cycle for Delhi to help runners pick optimal workout windows.
3. **Downloadable Citizen Air Quality Emergency Action PDF:**
   - Free one-page printable checklist for parents, schools, and offices on high-pollution days.
